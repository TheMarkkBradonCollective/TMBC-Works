Par TV Services is a mobile, in-home TV repair business serving Sacramento and surrounding communities; its website lists a contact address in Davis. The site says the business has been in the field for 25 years and repairs 4K, LED, LCD, plasma, projection, DLP, direct-view CRT and digital TVs.

In-home service covers major brands including Samsung, Mitsubishi, Vizio, LG, Zenith, Sony, Hitachi, Toshiba and Panasonic, and the site describes the business as an authorized Mitsubishi service center. It says it stocks commonly needed parts, including TV lamps, and specializes in lamp replacement.

The site is clear about limits: it does not repair or replace TV screens or glass. It also explains that a diagnostic fee is credited toward the repair if you go ahead. The service area list includes Sacramento, Elk Grove, Citrus Heights, Roseville, Rancho Cordova, Folsom, Davis, West Sacramento and many other nearby cities.
