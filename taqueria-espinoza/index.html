The taqueria

Taqueria Espinoza is a family Mexican restaurant at 3671 Franklin Blvd in Sacramento.

The kitchen is known for street tacos with al pastor, carnitas, carne asada, suadero, tripas and buche, plus crispy sopes, tortas, taquitos and burritos. Soups are a draw too: menudo, birria, pozole and caldo de pollo or de res.

Reviewers call it one of their favorite taco spots in Sacramento, with friendly service, a clean dining room and good value. Takeout available and credit cards accepted. Call (916) 370-2511.
