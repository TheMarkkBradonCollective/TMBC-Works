Nisei Barber Shop has been cutting hair at 1505 4th St since about 1950. It is the last surviving business of Sacramento's original Japantown, which the city cleared and redeveloped after the World War II incarceration of Japanese Americans.

Founder Jack Umezu opened the shop after serving in the U.S. Army in World War II. Attorney Henry Taketa, one of Sacramento's early nisei (second-generation Japanese Americans), put up the building and leased Jack the space.

Today Jack's grandson, Cory Umezu, runs the two-chair shop. Some of his grandfather's longtime clients still come in, alongside downtown workers. With only two chairs, it is best to call ahead.
