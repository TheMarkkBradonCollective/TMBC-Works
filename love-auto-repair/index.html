About Love Auto Repair

Love Auto Repair is an auto repair shop at 3523 Kiessig Ave, Suite J, in South Sacramento. Owner Tony has been in South Sac for more than 30 years and took over the shop in 2004, according to the business's Yelp profile.

The shop's Yelp listing lists routine automotive maintenance, and its directory listings describe general automotive repair. Tony describes the business as a small shop offering friendly service and fair prices.

Listings show cash and credit cards accepted. The shop is open Tuesday through Saturday with a midday break. Call (916) 391-4095 to describe your car's issue or book a visit.
