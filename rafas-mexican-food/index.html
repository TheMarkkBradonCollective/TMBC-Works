About Rafa's Mexican Food

Rafa's Mexican Food is a Mexican food truck that has served Natomas for years. Recent customer reports say the truck has moved, so call (916) 613-8145 to confirm where it is parked today.

Customer favorites listed on Yelp and Restaurantji include the breakfast burrito, carne asada fries, carnitas burrito, super burrito, chimichanga, chili verde burrito, street tacos, crispy and mini tacos, fish and shrimp tacos, quesadillas, taco salad and nachos.

Listings show takeout, catering and credit cards accepted.
