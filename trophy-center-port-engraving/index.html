Trophy Center & Port Engraving says it has provided over 65 years of local service. The shop recently moved, less than 10 minutes from its prior location, to 3609 Bradshaw Road, Suite G, half a mile south of Highway 50 at Old Placerville Road, between AutoZone and UPS.

Engraving is done on site with laser, rotary and diamond drag equipment, and assembly and manufacturing are also done on site. The website lists crystal and glass awards, plaques, acrylic and corporate awards, desk items and gifts, trophies and resins, medals and ribbons, promotional products, badges, signs, and electrical and industrial engraving.

Express service is listed at no extra cost. Call (916) 739-8624, or the after-hours mobile contact listed on the site, to ask about timing.
