About 59th Street Barbershop

59th Street Barbershop is at 2917 59th St in Sacramento. California's Board of Barbering and Cosmetology lists 59th Street Barbershop LLC as a current licensed barbershop establishment at this address.

The shop's own listing text calls it a clean, faster-paced barbershop that offers all types of services, including bald fades, flat tops, military cuts and shampoos, and says most haircuts are done with a vacuum for client comfort. It also notes the shop is open Sundays.
