Jay's Mobile Welding & Fabricating is a Sacramento welding and metal fabrication company at 8581 Younger Creek Dr, Suite 200. Its directory description says the company has been in business since 2005 and has over two decades of welding and fabricating experience.

The same description says it provides mobile welding as well as work in its shop, handles projects across California, and specializes in ADA-compliant fabrication such as hand and cane rails. It names quality, safety and design as the focus of every project.

Listings show weekday hours from 8 AM to 5 PM. Because the shop is described as working with some customers by arrangement, call (916) 203-8082 to confirm what the shop is taking on and whether mobile work is available for your job.
