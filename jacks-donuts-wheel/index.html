Jack's Donuts Wheel is a donut shop at 2261 Northgate Blvd in Sacramento. Restaurantji describes it as a mom-and-pop shop that has been a local favorite since 1992.

Listings show glazed donuts, iced rolls, donut holes and donut sticks, along with filled donuts and croissant breakfast sandwiches. Coffee is listed as Peerless.

Hours differ between listings, so call (916) 925-6619 to confirm when the shop is open before you go.
