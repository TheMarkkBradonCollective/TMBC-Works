Chang's Thai Cuisine serves Thai dishes from 3620 N Freeway Blvd, Suite 310, in Sacramento. Its website says the key to its success is quality, consistent food that tastes great every time, and that the restaurant is Halal certified for its chicken and beef dishes.

The restaurant offers online ordering for pickup and delivery, and its site says ordering direct saves money. An afternoon special runs Tuesday through Friday from 4:30 to 6:00 PM: buy two entrees and get free potstickers or a fried roll and a free Thai tea or soda.

Hours are listed on the website for Tuesday through Sunday, with a midafternoon break Tuesday through Friday. Call (916) 333-2978 or order online.
