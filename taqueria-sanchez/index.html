Taqueria Sanchez is a Mexican restaurant at 2885 Norwood Ave in North Sacramento. Listings show a compact menu built around tacos, burritos, quesadillas and gorditas, with outdoor dining and a parking lot.

Tacos come in options such as al pastor and carne asada, and listings mention radish, grilled serrano peppers and onions served alongside. Specialties include a super burrito, machetes (two-foot-long quesadillas made with homemade tortillas) and gorditas de chicharron prensado.

Apple Maps lists takeout, delivery, Apple Pay and credit cards. Hours are consistent across listings: open Tuesday through Sunday, closed Monday.
