I Love My Shoeshine offers shoeshine service in downtown Sacramento at 824 L St (Sutter Club Garage) and mobile service for events.

Its website says the founder shined shoes for more than 20 years at Sacramento's international airport before opening downtown locations.

Mobile service is offered for parties, trade shows, meetings, weddings, golf tournaments, conventions, and appreciation days. Gift certificates are available.
