Shoe shinesMobile shoeshine for eventsWeddings and conventionsCorporate and customer appreciation events

I Love My Shoeshine offers professional shoe shines at 824 L St in downtown Sacramento, at the Sutter Club Garage. The owner spent more than 20 years shining shoes for travelers at Sacramento's airport before opening her own downtown locations, according to the business's website and Sacramento Bee coverage it links to.

The website lists the L Street location on weekdays at midday and says appointments are recommended. Earlier it also listed Wednesday visits at the Matsui Federal Courthouse, which has since moved to the L Street location.

The business also offers mobile shoeshine service for events including parties, trade shows, meetings, weddings, golf tournaments, conventions and customer appreciation days, and sells gift certificates. Call (916) 213-7723 for quotes and bookings.
