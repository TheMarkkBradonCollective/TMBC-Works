Aluminum & Stainless Weldmasters is a specialty welding shop at 1621 Juliesse Ave, Suite B, in Sacramento. Its website describes the shop as thin-material specialists who repair almost any part made from aluminum, stainless, titanium, brass, cast iron and exotic metals.

The site lists X-ray quality welding in the shop by appointment only with quick turnaround, prototype products, small to large production runs and emergency repairs, and says no job is too small.

Past work listed on the site includes aircraft parts, cracked aluminum wheels, vintage car parts and fenders, motorcycle and bicycle frames, aluminum boats and trailers, transmission cases, manifolds, heads, radiators and oil pans. The About page says the shop has more than 30 years in welding and has references that sub-contract from aircraft manufacturers.
