About Power Inn Auto

Power Inn Auto is an independent auto repair shop at 7117 Power Inn Rd in Sacramento.

It handles diagnostics, routine maintenance, suspension work and engine replacement, including installing engines customers supply. Reviewers name Ben, the mechanic, and say he shows you the parts he replaces.

Customers say they have been coming for 15 years or more, praising fair prices, quick diagnosis and no unnecessary repairs. Parking lot on site. Call (916) 386-1964.
