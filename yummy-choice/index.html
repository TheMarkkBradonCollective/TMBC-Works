Yummy Choice is a small, family-run lunch counter at 1601 10th St in downtown Sacramento, near 10th and P and the state office buildings.

The menu mixes Vietnamese, Chinese and American dishes: banh mi, shrimp spring rolls, teriyaki chicken, fried rice, Vietnamese beef stew, wonton soup, potstickers, burgers and fries. Listings mention counter service, an upstairs dining area and an outdoor patio.

It is open weekdays for lunch only, and reviewers say the line moves quickly. Call (916) 341-0298.
