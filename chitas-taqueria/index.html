Chita's Taqueria has been serving Sacramento since 2006 from a small, cozy spot at 2019 Q St, which its website calls a hole-in-the-wall with local artwork on the walls. Seating ranges from counter spots to small tables.

The restaurant's own website highlights its breakfast burritos, seasoned al pastor, creamy beans and expertly prepared eggs. Tortas can be customized with thin-sliced ham or chorizo.

The website does not list hours, so call (916) 930-0951 before you go.
