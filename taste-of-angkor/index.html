About Taste of Angkor

Taste of Angkor is a Cambodian restaurant at 4566 Mack Rd in South Sacramento. In its own words on Yelp, it is proud to be the first Cambodian restaurant in Sacramento to serve primarily Cambodian cuisine, along with other Asian dishes and noodle soups.

The restaurant describes Cambodian food as built on fresh vegetables, herbs and grilling, eaten as several dishes at once so the table gets sweet, sour, salty and spicy flavors, with chili left for each diner to add.

Popular dishes listed on Yelp include papaya salad, stuffed chicken wings, beef skewers, Cambodian beef sausages, beef laab, Cambodian pho and sour beef soup.
