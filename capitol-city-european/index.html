Capitol City European is a European car service center at 4150 Power Inn Rd, Unit 2, in Sacramento. The owner started it in fall 2001, according to the About page, and the services page focuses on Mercedes-Benz and BMW repair.

The site lists computer diagnostics, factory-recommended maintenance, electrical repairs, heating and air conditioning, brake and suspension repairs and more. It says repairs are explained clearly and backed by a 2-year or 24,000-mile warranty.

Service categories on the site include air conditioning, fuel system, cooling and heating, steering and suspension, brakes, exhaust, oil and fluid services, scheduled 15k to 90k services, and electrical diagnostics. The shop is open weekdays 8 AM to 5 PM; call (916) 452-5811.
