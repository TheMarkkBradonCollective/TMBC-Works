El Novillero opened in 1970 at 4216 Franklin Blvd, on the corner of 18th Avenue, as a 28-seat restaurant. The Davalos family says it has grown into one of Sacramento's best-known Mexican restaurants, now seating 180. The name means The Apprentice Bullfighter.

The restaurant's menu notes say the family's patriarch opened several Mexican restaurants in downtown Sacramento before El Novillero, serving huevos rancheros, chile verde and carnitas, and that carnitas became his specialty. Carnitas de puerco, roasted pork served several ways, is listed on the menu as the house specialty.

The kitchen makes everything fresh daily, from the sauces to the corn chips. The menu notes that lard is used to prepare dishes, and a vegetarian information section lists which items are meat-free and which sauces contain pork, peanuts or gluten.

The restaurant has its own 127-space lot, does not take reservations, and accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover. Alcohol is served only with food.

  • Carnitas dinner platter with rice, beans, avocado slices and pico de gallo

  • Gallina a la tampiquena
  • Chimichanga grande
  • Huevos rancheros and chorizo con huevos
  • Enchiladas rancheras
