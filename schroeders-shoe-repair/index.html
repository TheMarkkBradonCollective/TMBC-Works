About Schroeder's Shoe Repair

Schroeder's Shoe Repair is a long-running shoe and boot repair shop at 2022 10th St in Sacramento. Its Yahoo Local listing describes complete shoe and boot repair with one-day service available.

CBS Sacramento's roundup of local cobblers noted that the shop can shorten a belt or add new holes, replace heel and toe tips, clean and refresh footwear, and sell laces for almost any type of shoe. Directory listings also mention repairs on boots, hiking boots, designer shoes and bags, including zipper and sole work.

The shop takes cash or check only, per CBS Sacramento and recent listings. Call (916) 441-1912 before you come in with a repair you are unsure about.
