Larson Industrial Services, Inc. is a Sacramento-based industrial millwright and piping contractor at 6000 Midway St, Suite 400, serving much of Northern California. Its website says it can man any size job with trained professionals in industrial construction.

The site's specialties are welding services, material handling, machinery installation, food processing, emergency services, engineered quality, and pipes and piping. Work listed includes mobile welding, conveyor installation and maintenance, stainless steel piping and tubing, sanitary welding, shut-down support and plant maintenance.

The company's stated values are safety, customer satisfaction, teamwork and quality workmanship. Call (916) 383-6022 to discuss an industrial project.
