About Elegant Designs

Elegant Designs is a tailoring and alterations shop at 2326 Fair Oaks Blvd, Suite F, in Sacramento, run by Jenny for nearly 20 years. It is Jenny's small shop, and Jenny handles fittings and alterations personally.

Its Yelp listing shows clothing alterations, skirts and dresses, and clothing mending and repair, and many reviewers mention wedding dress alterations and bustles.

Listings show credit cards and Apple Pay accepted, and the business is listed as women-owned. Call (916) 921-1221 to set up a fitting.
