About Sara's Alterations

Sara's Alterations is a family-run alterations and tailoring shop inside Country Club Plaza at 2380 Watt Ave, Suite 350.

In the shop's own listing description, it is skilled in altering leather jackets, coats, vests and furs, bridal and bridesmaid dresses, tuxedos, jeans and dress pants, as well as Halloween costumes. Reviewers mention hemming jeans with the original hem.

Recent reviews say the shop is cash only. Call (916) 480-2959.
