Friends Of Bailee is a dog grooming studio at 6622 Freeport Blvd, #4, in Sacramento, across from Bing Maloney Golf Course. Its website says it aims to create a calm environment by booking each dog its own appointment time, with lots of praise, patience and one-on-one attention.

The grooming menu starts with a Bath, which includes brush-out, bath with personalized shampoo and conditioner, blow dry, nail trim, ear cleaning, a bow or bandana, and gland expression by request on dogs 30 pounds and up. Bath & Trim adds a sanitary trim, paw pad trim and shaping of the legs, feet and hocks, and a Full Groom adds a cut and style to fit your dog's lifestyle.

The site lists add-ons such as nail trim, ear cleaning, gland expression and teeth brushing. Policies include 24 hours notice for cancellations, current rabies vaccinations, and arriving on time. Call or text (916) 662-4942 to book.
